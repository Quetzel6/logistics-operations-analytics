Definitions and limitations

On-time and OTIF denominators include all orders due on or before the as-of date, including overdue undelivered orders. OTIF requires both on-time delivery and all ordered units shipped. Unit fill is shipped / ordered units for due orders. Trip utilization is shipped units / capacity summed once per dispatched trip; units are homogeneous capacity units, not weight or vehicle volume. The dispatch window is 28 days; no real GPS, labor, cost, inventory or causal evidence is included.
